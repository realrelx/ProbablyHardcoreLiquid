Liquids never pour out completely again: every container keeps an inherent residue, and pouring, transferring or draining only moves (total − residue). The residue and the poured part keep the exact same composition; a bottle with only residue left cannot be poured at all until fresh liquid is added.

Residue = container capacity × residue percent × viscosity factor, capped by a configurable limit. Percent, cap, container scope and viscosity scaling are all freely editable in the settings menu; a real-viscosity table covers all 37 vanilla liquid components (water = 1: alcohol 1.2, cooking oil 80, starch slurry 100, lye 13…), and liquids from other mods default to water. Declares zero capabilities and never touches nightly machine flows.

Config (defaults): residue_percent = 0.5 (0 = off) · max_residue_ml = 10 (0 = no cap) · container_scope = all / vanilla · viscosity_scale = 1 (0 = viscosity off).

Safe & open source: the whole mod is fully open source — every line of code is public and auditable, it declares zero capabilities and contains no hidden behavior. Review the source yourself before subscribing: https://github.com/realrelx/ProbablyHardcoreLiquid

液体再也倒不干净了：每个容器都有"固有残留"，倾倒、转移、清空都只能带走 总量 − 残留，留下的那一点和倒出去的部分成分完全同比例；瓶里只剩残留时怎么倒都倒不出来，必须兑入新液体才能再倒。

残留大小由容器容积 × 残留比例 × 粘度因子决定（受残留上限封顶），比例、上限、容器范围、粘度缩放全部可在设置菜单自由输入调整；内置覆盖原版全部 37 种液体成分的真实粘度表（水=1：酒精 1.2、烹饪油 80、淀粉糊 100、浓烧碱 13……），其他 mod 的液体默认按水算，天然兼容。零权限声明，不影响原版机器流程。菜单文字随游戏语言自动切换（默认中文 / English）。

配置（默认值）：residue_percent = 0.5（0 = 关闭）· max_residue_ml = 10（0 = 不设上限）· container_scope = all / vanilla · viscosity_scale = 1（0 = 关闭粘度）。
